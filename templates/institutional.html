Questions

Frequently asked

How is the price set?

Each contract is quoted for your institution: how many models and how much storage your members need for the year. You pay the annual contract by invoice.

Who can join our institution?

Anyone your panel admins invite with a link. If you give us your email domains, only accounts with a confirmed address on those domains can accept. Each account belongs to one institution.

Can members keep their models private?

Yes. A model follows its project's visibility. Private models count toward your quota and appear in your panel, but never on the public showcase.

What happens when the contract ends?

Viewers and QR codes show a clear notice instead of a broken link. When you renew, every funded model is extended at once, with the same links and QR codes.

Can we try it before we sign?

You can explore the demo institution panel right now.
