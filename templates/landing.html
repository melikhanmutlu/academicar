Augmented Reality

Place 3D models in the real world

Readers scan the QR code from your publication and instantly place the 3D model on any surface using their phone camera. No app download needed — it works directly in the mobile browser via WebXR.

  • Mobile-native AR iOS & Android browsers, powered by WebXR
  • True-to-scale placement Models render at real dimensions on any surface
  • QR-powered access One scan from the printed paper opens AR instantly
