This Agreement governs the online sale of digital license upgrades for 3D/AR model hosting on AcademicAR — single-model plans (for example Academic or Extended Archive access) shown on the Pricing page — purchased by card through our payment service provider, in accordance with applicable distance-selling and consumer-protection law. 4. Payment Method

Payment is collected by card through iyzico, our payment service provider. AcademicAR does not receive or store full card numbers; iyzico processes the transaction on its own PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure. 5. Delivery / Performance

AcademicAR delivers a digital license, not a physical product. Performance is immediate: once iyzico confirms payment as successful, the purchased license is applied to the Customer's model automatically, updating its access window, storage limit, and viewer watermark status. No shipping address, carrier, or delivery time applies.

6. Right of Withdrawal

Consumers ordinarily have a 14-day right of withdrawal from a distance contract. Because the Customer requests immediate activation and expressly acknowledges losing the withdrawal right once performance begins, that right ends as soon as the license is applied, consistent with the digital-content/service exception recognized under applicable distance-selling regulations. See the Delivery & Refund Policy for how refunds are handled outside the withdrawal right, such as duplicate charges or activation failures.

AcademicAR will apply the purchased license to the specified model promptly upon confirmed payment, keep the model's viewer link and QR resolver stable through the license window as described in the Terms of Use, and handle payment data only through its PCI-DSS compliant payment service provider.
