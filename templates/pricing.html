How billing maps to publishing

A plan follows the model, not the whole account.

01Create a project

Start with context and models; DOI/PMID fields and documents remain optional.

02Upload models

Each GLB, STL, OBJ, FBX, STEP or medical-scan asset becomes its own model registry item.

03Choose access

Select Free, Academic, or Extended per model based on how long it should stay active.

04Share durable links

Use the project page, review link, model viewer, QR resolver, screenshots, and mobile AR entry point.
