Common questions

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay once per model, and the model stays online for its plan's access window. Your account and projects cost nothing.

Can I start free and upgrade later?

Yes. Upgrade a model from its page at any time. The viewer link and the QR code you already printed keep working.

What happens when a plan ends?

Visitors see a notice that the model is unavailable rather than a broken page, and you can renew it from the model's page.

Can I replace the model file?

Yes. Upload a new version and it takes the old one's place behind the same link and QR code.
