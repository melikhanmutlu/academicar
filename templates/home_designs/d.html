Interlocks for patient-derived data

Scans are handled differently from other uploads. These rules are built into the upload and conversion flow, not left to a setting.

Read the DICOM to 3D guide
  • Confirmation on every uploadAnonymisation, rights and ethics are confirmed for every model and every replacement, with an audit record.
  • An extra check for scansDICOM and segmentation uploads need a second, medical-specific confirmation.
  • Raw scan deletedThe uploaded scan is removed after conversion, whether it succeeds or fails. Medical uploads are never archived or mirrored.
  • No identifiers in logsPatient identifiers are never written to logs. Models are for education and presentation, not diagnosis.
