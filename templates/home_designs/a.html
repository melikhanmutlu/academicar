3D and AR for research, teaching and publishing

Publish the model, not a screenshot of it.

Upload GLB, STL, OBJ, FBX, STEP or a CT/MR scan. AcademicAR turns it into a browser viewer, an AR view and a stable QR code for your paper, poster or thesis.

Start publishing Open the live example
  1. Orbit

    Rotate it in any browser

    Orbit, zoom and inspect from every side. No login, no app, no plug-in for your readers.

  2. AR

    Scan the QR, place it on the desk

    Every model gets a stable QR code. Phones open it in AR straight from the browser.

  3. Section

    Cut a section, read the slice

    Move a section plane along any axis and read the 2D cross-section with a snapping measure.

  4. Figure

    Measure, label, export the figure

    Add distances and labels, then export a PNG figure with a layer legend, scale bar and QR.
