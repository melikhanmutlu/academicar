1Introduction

Most 3D research still reaches readers as a flat screenshot, captured at one angle and pasted into a PDF. Depth, scale and the relation between parts are left to the imagination.1 AcademicAR lets the model itself travel with the work.

1A static figure cannot be turned, cut or measured.

2Methods

A background worker turns every input into a compressed GLB with a USDZ companion for iPhone AR. Multi-part models keep one named layer per part or structure.2

2Medical scans need an extra confirmation, and the raw scan is deleted after conversion.

Readers get tools that used to need desktop software, in the browser.
