Medical scan

What to extract *

Used for a DICOM series (ZIP). Pick several to get one layer per structure; you can fade each layer in the viewer. Choosing differently later means uploading the scan again, because the raw scan is deleted after processing. A segmentation (NIfTI, NRRD, DICOM-SEG, or a ZIP of masks) keeps its own structures, one layer each.
